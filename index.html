📖 HOW TO COMMAND OUTPOST ALPHA

1. The Core Objective

Survive 30 simulated lunar days (Sols) with all four astronauts alive and outpost systems sustainable.

2. Resource Balancing

Every system is interconnected. Upgrading the Greenhouse increases food production, but consumes extra electricity and water. Upgrading Water Recovery reduces net water loss, but draws battery power. If battery drops to zero, life support and heaters suffer a catastrophic brownout!

3. Operating Modes

In the Engineering Bay, toggle facility modes: switch ECLSS or Water Recovery into ECO mode to survive energy deficits, or BOOST mode when reserves drop dangerously low.

4. Emergencies & Crises

Every few Sols, a dynamic space crisis triggers (solar storms, micrometeorite strikes, dust adhesion). Choose your engineering directive carefully—each choice requires transparent trade-offs between materials, energy, and crew safety.

5. Science Behind the Mission

Whenever an engineering dilemma arises, open the Science Archive to discover the real aerospace principles used by NASA and international space exploration initiatives.
